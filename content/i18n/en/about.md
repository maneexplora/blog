Hi, I'm **Manuel Alemán**.

I'm from **Ojocaliente**, a small town in Zacatecas, Mexico, and I've lived in several parts of the country. Maybe that's why I value it so much when a place shows **quality, authenticity and, above all, warmth** toward its guests.

I work in **global Supply Chain**. Thanks to my job, over the last year I've been able to travel constantly, and in every conversation people ask me the same thing: *where did you go? what do you recommend? what was the best thing you ate?* That's how Mane Explora was born: a hobby to share my opinion about the places I visit, built with the same structure and standards I use at work every day.

I've been lucky enough to eat in different parts of the world, but I'll always remember a place in **El Cielo, in Gómez Farías**, Tamaulipas. A guide took us to a lady who cooked exquisitely. The place was humble but warm, and the pork asado with rice she served us was something else. That meal sums up what I look for in every visit.

## My method {#metodo}

I rate from **1 to 5 stars**, with half points, just like Google or TripAdvisor. The overall rating is a **weighted average**: each criterion counts according to its importance, and the result is rounded to the nearest half point.

**Restaurants and cafés**

| Order | Criterion | Weight | What I evaluate |
|:-:|---|:-:|---|
| 1 | Service | 35% | Being welcomed warmly and well looked after |
| 2 | Flavor | 30% | The heart of any meal |
| 3 | Ambience and presentation | 15% | The place and how the dishes arrive at the table |
| 4 | Value for money | 20% | Whether what you pay matches what you get |

On value for money, as my mother used to say: *how nice it is to spend when you enjoy the food, and how awful when you pay a lot and didn't enjoy it.*

**Museums and culture**

| Order | Criterion | Weight | What I evaluate |
|:-:|---|:-:|---|
| 1 | Exhibition | 35% | The quality and interest of the works |
| 2 | Experience and curation | 20% | How the visit is presented and experienced |
| 3 | Building | 15% | Architecture, facilities and comfort |
| 4 | Guidance and assistance | 15% | Information, signage and guides |
| 5 | Value for money | 15% | What the ticket is worth |

Museums end with a verdict:

| Rating | Verdict |
|:-:|---|
| 4.5 to 5 | Worth the trip |
| 3.5 to 4 | Worth a detour |
| Below 3.5 | Interesting |

Every review includes quick facts with the approximate spend, whether you should book and what occasion it's ideal for.

## Transparency

I write in the first person, although I often share what *we* liked most, meaning the people who come with me. I pay for my own visits. If a visit is ever by invitation, I'll say so clearly at the start of the review.
