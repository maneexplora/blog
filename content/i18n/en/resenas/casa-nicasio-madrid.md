One night we had dinner at Casa Nicasio, a traditional restaurant in central Madrid. We were struck by how many people from other countries were there: we heard Italian, Russian and other languages at the tables around us.

## What we ordered

- **Garlic soup (sopa de ajo)**, served in a clay pot.
- **Migas** with chorizo and pepper.
- **A meat and onion sauté.**
- **Beer**, plus bread with olive oil.

## The good

- **The service.** Very good: they were very attentive throughout dinner.
- **The food.** Everything was tasty, the beer too.

## Could be better

- **The flavour.** Very tasty, but not unbeatable.
- **The atmosphere.** Fine, that of a busy restaurant in the centre.

## A bit of history

The name comes from grandfather Nicasio, and the house describes itself as using ingredients from the countryside and the vegetable garden in traditional Madrid recipes.

*Source: [Salir.com](https://www.salir.com/casa-nicasio-madrid-neg-135977.html).*

## Verdict

A good place to try classic Madrid food with very good service. I'd go back.
