This site does not ask you to register or provide personal data. Your browser may share basic technical information (such as your device type or the pages you visit) with the services described below.

## Cookies and advertising

This site may show **Google AdSense** ads. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalized advertising in [Google's ad settings](https://adssettings.google.com).

More information on how Google uses data: [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Visit statistics

I use **Metricool** to count visits and see which pages are read and from which country. Its tracking does not use cookies.

Reviews include Google Maps. When they load, Google may collect data according to its own privacy policy.
