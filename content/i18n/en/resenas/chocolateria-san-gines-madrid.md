That same night, after the Banco de España, we went for chocolate con churros at San Ginés, Madrid's most classic chocolatería.

## What we ordered

- **Chocolate con churros** for two: one portion of churros and two cups of chocolate.

## The good

- **The place.** The passageway, the entrance and the history: it's one of the most classic things to do in Madrid.
- **The service.** Good.
- **The atmosphere.** There are tables outside; we sat there and enjoyed it.
- **No queue for us,** although lots of people started arriving right after we did.

## Could be better

- **The flavour.** It wasn't that tasty. We've tried other chocolaterías since and liked their chocolate con churros more.
- **Value for money.** The price is in line with the usual in the centre, but for that quality we'd look elsewhere.

## Price

We paid €10.40 for two. For reference, a portion of churros with chocolate costs about €4–5 in 2026 at most churrerías in the centre, San Ginés included, according to [The Better Vacation](https://thebettervacation.com/madrid/best-churros-in-madrid/); [La Maleta Inquieta](https://lamaletainquieta.com/churros-chocolate-madrid/) gives a €4–6 range and notes that Chocolatería Valor tends to be a bit pricier.

## A bit of history

San Ginés has been in the Pasadizo de San Ginés since 1894. From the start it was where theatre audiences went after the show, and it appears in works such as Benito Pérez Galdós' *Episodios Nacionales* and Valle-Inclán's *Luces de bohemia*.

*Source: [esMadrid](https://w3.esmadrid.com/en/print/pdf/node/2057).*

## Verdict

I'd go back, but only to take someone to see the classic. For flavour, we'd look for other chocolaterías.
