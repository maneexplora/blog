We got to Parque Warner almost at 3 pm and that day it closed at 8, so we only had five hours. Even so, we loved it.

## The show: 5 out of 5

We watched the whole **DC Super-Villains Stunt Show**, a stunt show on wheels: the Joker, the Riddler, Harley Quinn and the Suicide Squad appear, but it's all about cars and motorbikes. It's very well staged. It was the only show we saw in full and I give it 5 out of 5. It's new: it premiered on 1 July 2026 at the Teatro Especialistas, replacing *Police Academy*. *(Source: OKDiario.)*

## The rides: 4 out of 5

- **Batman Gotham City Escape**, our favourite. The theming starts in the queue: you walk into Wayne Manor, through the study and on to the platform. I thought it would be a gentle ride, but no: it's pure adrenaline, and themed all the way to the end. It's an Intamin multi-launch coaster with three launches, a 45 m top height and 104 km/h, opened in May 2023. *(Sources: Blooloop, Samsung Members.)*
- **Superman: La Atracción de Acero**, the park's floorless coaster.
- **Arkham Asylum**: the Gotham asylum theming is great and the ride is good.
- **Stunt Fall**, near the entrance: one of the most intense, the kind that rattles your head.
- **Coaster Express**, the wooden coaster. The park bills it as the tallest and longest in Europe (1,250 m), but we didn't enjoy it: everything shakes, it's pure vibration. If you have lower-back or neck problems, you may have a bad time. *(Source: Parque Warner.)*

I give the rides a 4 because, apart from Batman, most are fairly standard.

## The food: the biggest opportunity

That day the food wasn't good. In the end, very hungry, we bought hot dogs (*perritos* here): the bun was hard and cold, and they don't even heat the sausage. The ice cream, though, was really good.

## The parade

We finished with the Bugs Bunny and Looney Tunes parade. It was fun, but it left me wanting more: about 10 floats at most. For a park this size I expected something longer.

## Leaving

Getting out was a struggle too: the bus filled up completely and we had to order a Bolt to the Renfe station to get back to Madrid.

## In short

A really good, lovely experience. If you go, arrive early to catch more shows, prioritise Batman Gotham City Escape and the villains' show, and bring something to eat.
