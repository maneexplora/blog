Our way to Madrid started with a layover in Houston. We flew out of Terminal A at George Bush Intercontinental Airport (IAH), gate A12, on a Delta flight to Atlanta.

## A bit of history

The airport opened on June 8, 1969 as Houston Intercontinental Airport, with Terminals A and B connected by an underground train. In 1997 it was renamed in honor of President George H. W. Bush. *Source: [Houston Airports](https://www.fly2houston.com/airport-business/newsroom/articles/item/iah-celebrates-55-years-and-honors-president-bushs-centennial/).*

## The airport's art

Houston Airports has a collection of nearly 450 works across its terminals, and Skytrax named it the World's Best Airport Art Program in 2023, 2024 and 2025. *Source: [Houston Airports](https://www.fly2houston.com/iah/world-class-art/).*

Here's what we found on our walk:

- **Greetings from Houston**, by Daniel Anguilu, a Mexican American artist from Houston. Houston Airports describes it as one of the most photographed artworks at IAH. *Source: [Houston Airports](https://www.fly2houston.com/newsroom/articles/new-mural-unveiled-outside-mothers-room-and-sensory-room-bush-airport).*
- **A hummingbird**, a few steps away, above the massage-chair area.
- **The rodeo exhibit**, with painted horses, a saddle and a wagon. Houston's airports keep rodeo exhibits up year-round, with rotating pieces loaned by RodeoHouston. *Source: [Axios Houston](https://www.axios.com/local/houston/2023/03/21/rodeo-art-houston-airports).*
- **A neon rocket**, near the gates.

## The good

- **Wayfinding.** Getting to our gate was easy and the flight left on time.
- **The art we saw.** Anguilu's mural and the rodeo exhibit make the wait more interesting.

## Room for improvement

- **Art in our area.** For such an award-winning program, we found few works on our walk through Terminal A. There's likely more in other terminals, so it's worth checking what's in yours ahead of time.
- **Comfort, food and shops.** They did the job without standing out.

## Verdict

A smooth layover, easy to get around, with a couple of artworks worth seeking out. If you have time between flights, take a detour to the Greetings from Houston mural.
