One midday, before heading to the Warner Bros. park, we stopped at Manolo Bakes, the chain famous for its manolitos.

## What we ordered

- **A Biscoff-filled manolo** and **a pistachio one**: €3.40 each, according to the display.
- **An iced matcha** and **a café con leche.**

## The good

- **The presentation.** Very pretty: the display, the pastries and the shop are carefully styled.

## Could be better

- **The flavour.** It didn't win us over.
- **Value for money.** We paid about €15 for two and, for the flavour, it felt expensive.
- **The service.** Ordinary.

## My take

I was telling my girlfriend that some businesses are focusing so much on making everything look pretty that flavour gets left behind. In the end, flavour is what satisfies the customer. If you put that much care into looks, give taste the same weight.

## A bit of history

Manolo Bakes was born in December 2017 and opened its first shop in Madrid in June 2018. It's known for its manolitos and palmitas, baked every day and decorated by hand.

*Source: [Muelle Uno](https://www.muelleuno.com/en/?p=14149).*

## Verdict

Great for a photo; for a craving, I'd look elsewhere. This was our experience that day at that branch.
