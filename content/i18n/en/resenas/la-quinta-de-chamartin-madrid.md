On one of our first days in Madrid we had dinner at La Quinta de Chamartín, a tapas and raciones restaurant on Calle de Colombia.

## What we ordered

- **Betanzos-style tortilla with Padrón peppers.** Delicious, one of the best I've had in Madrid.
- **Homemade croquetas.** Very good.
- **Two tostas with fries:** one with Iberian ham and one with salmon. Very tasty, with big portions.
- **To drink:** the house red wine, which was very good, and a couple of beers.

With our drinks, the house brought us a tosta we hadn't ordered, and a couple of glasses of white wine we hadn't ordered also arrived.

## The good

- **The flavor.** Everything was very tasty, starting with the tortilla.
- **The portions.** Very big: by the end we were completely full.
- **Value for money.** We paid €53.30 for two, drinks included, which didn't feel expensive for how well we ate.
- **The house wine.** Very good.

## Room for improvement

- **The service.** They treated us well, but without much attention or anything out of the ordinary.
- **The atmosphere.** A typical neighborhood restaurant, nothing special.

## Useful info from the menu

According to the menu, the kitchen is open from 9:00 to 22:35 and the last table is seated at 22:15. The house recommendations are marked with a heart; the Betanzos-style tortilla with Padrón peppers is one of them.

## Verdict

A place to eat very well and plentifully at a good price. Service and atmosphere are fine, but the food is what makes it worth the visit. I'd go back.
